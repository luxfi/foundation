Our Mission

Building the Infrastructure for a Decentralized Future

The Lux Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to fostering the growth and adoption of decentralized technologies. We believe in a future where individuals have greater control over their data, assets, and digital interactions.

Through strategic grants, cutting-edge research, comprehensive education, and open-source development, we're building the foundation for Web3 and beyond.

Our work spans consensus mechanisms, post-quantum cryptography, cross-chain interoperability, and developer tooling - all designed to make decentralized technology more accessible, secure, and scalable.
